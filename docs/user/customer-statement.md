# Customer Statement

Open the **Customer Statement** report from the Selling sidebar, or **View → Customer Statement** on a Customer.

- Opening balance, then invoices, payments and returns with a running balance, and ageing.
- **Statement PDF** renders the `Customer Statement` print format with the issuer's logo, address, tax ID and contacts from the Company, and the first company Bank Account as payment details.
- Period and company are passed as `from_date`, `to_date` and `company`.
