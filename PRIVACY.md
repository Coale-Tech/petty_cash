# Privacy Policy

**Petty Cash** for ERPNext, published by Coale Tech (`support@coale.tech`). Last updated: 13 October 2026.

## 1. Summary
Petty Cash does not collect, transmit or share any data with Coale Tech or any third party. All data stays in your own Frappe site.

## 2. What the app stores
Inside your site's database only, as ordinary ERPNext records:
- Petty Cash Float settings: company, cost centre, accounts, imprest amount and expense templates.
- Vouchers created through the app: Journal Entries, Payment Entries and their remarks, receipt references, supplier and customer names, and amounts.
- The usernames Frappe already records on those documents (creator, modifier, timestamps).

The app adds no tracking, cookies, analytics or advertising identifiers of its own. Browser sessions are the standard Frappe session cookies.

## 3. External requests
The app makes no external network requests. We verified that its Python and JavaScript contain no calls to external services, telemetry or analytics endpoints.

## 4. Who can see your data
Only users of your site with the relevant ERPNext permissions. The Petty Cash Custodian role, Accounts User, Accounts Manager and System Manager roles decide access, and User Permissions on Cost Center can limit a user to one float. Coale Tech has no access to your site or its data.

If your site is hosted on Frappe Cloud, Frappe Technologies Pvt. Ltd. hosts and processes the site under its own privacy policy. Coale Tech is not involved in that processing.

## 5. Support communications
If you email `support@coale.tech` or open a GitHub issue, we receive what you send us (email address, message and any attachments). We use it only to answer you and improve the app, and we do not sell or share it. Do not post real financial data or credentials in public issues.

## 6. Retention and deletion
You control retention. Uninstalling the app removes its Customer Statement sidebar link but leaves your accounting records, as ERPNext requires them for your books. Delete or anonymise records according to your own legal retention rules. To remove data we hold from a support exchange, email `support@coale.tech`.

## 7. Your responsibilities
You are the controller of the personal and financial data in your site and are responsible for the lawful basis, notices, retention and security that the laws of your country require, such as the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, the GDPR or similar.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The version published in the repository when you install or update applies.

## 9. Contact
Coale Tech: `support@coale.tech`.
